# Third-party notices

## pdfcn

The look of the PDF layouts in `render/` (the zinc greys, the type scale, the
small labels, the table and totals styles) follows the invoice designs of
[pdfcn](https://github.com/shadcn-labs/pdfcn), read at commit
`39c75c1abbbad7b89ad1d8d3ea740ef635818a4b`. No pdfcn file is included in this
repository: its designs are re-expressed in our own pdfmake code. `classic`
takes after invoice-classic, `modern` after invoice-modern, `compact` after
invoice-minimal and `letterhead` after invoice-corporate; the notes callout
takes after invoice-consultant.
